# SECFetcher Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 12, 2026

SECFetcher is a browser extension for finding, opening, and downloading public SEC filings by ticker symbol, including periodic reports, material event reports, proxy statements, and insider ownership filings.

## Data Collection

SECFetcher does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal information.

The extension does not use analytics, advertising trackers, account login, or remote telemetry.

## Local Storage

SECFetcher may store the latest filing results locally in your browser so the popup can restore the previous results when reopened. This information stays on your device and is not transmitted to the developer.

## Network Requests

When you search for a ticker, SECFetcher sends requests to public SEC endpoints, including `sec.gov` and `data.sec.gov`, to retrieve public company and filing data.

When you open or download a filing, your browser requests that filing directly from `sec.gov`.

## Permissions

SECFetcher requests only the permissions needed for its core function:

- `storage`: save recent popup results locally in the browser.
- `downloads`: download SEC filing documents when the user clicks Download.
- `https://www.sec.gov/*` and `https://data.sec.gov/*`: access public SEC filing data.

## Third Parties

SECFetcher uses public SEC data sources. It does not send user data to any analytics, advertising, or data broker services.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the support email listed on the SECFetcher Chrome Web Store listing.
